# Fonts

Both fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see the
`*-OFL.txt` files next to them). The licence allows bundling and embedding
in the shipped game; the font files must not be sold on their own.

| File | Font | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `CormorantGaramond.ttf` | Cormorant Garamond (variable weight), Christian Thalmann | Title, day cards, prologue text |
| `WorkSans.ttf` | Work Sans (variable weight), Wei Huang | UI, prompts, radio panel |

Source: github.com/google/fonts (ofl/cormorantgaramond, ofl/worksans).
Credit both in the game's credits screen (M7).
