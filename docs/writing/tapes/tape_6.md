### AI DRAFT (reference only)
```text
[Click. Wind in a cave. Metal on stone. A woman close to the microphone, a little out of breath.]
Dad. It's me. I'm in the cave again. I've swapped the battery. It's good for another winter, if the cold holds.
I signed it out as a spare for the Penobscot repeater. Don't laugh. If anyone counts, I'll go to prison for a battery.
I know what you'd say. You'd say leave it. You told me once the sea owed you something, and I've never known if you meant it as a debt or a warning.
The hydrophone picks up a ping every fourteen seconds, from the reef. Every one. I've timed it against the console clock. I tell myself it's her recorder. [Quieter.] I know it isn't.
The recorder's been dead since before I could read. It's the splice transponder, doing exactly what it was built to do.
But if I stop listening, Dad, then nobody is. Somebody has to be listening.
Walt's tape is still in the main hatch. I've never taken it out. It felt like reading his mail, and it felt like putting him out of the room.
So I'll leave mine here, under the service hatch, with the old battery. I can't keep it at the flat. It's too loud in there.
They're sending someone out for the winter. A man on his own, I heard. I hope it's someone who minds his own business. [A short laugh.] I'll be on sixteen at ten, like always.
That's all he'll get from me.
I'm twenty-seven years old, and I talk to a cave. It'll be three years in December.
[A breath.] I miss you. [Click.]
```
