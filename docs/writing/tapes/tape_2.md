### AI DRAFT (reference only)
```text
[Click. A hollow, ringing space. Dripping water. Boots on iron rungs.]
Field recorder, J. Haskell. Fourth of November, nineteen seventy-four. Oh-six-fifty-five, give or take my watch. Cable vault, south reef, splice number two.
Splice two has been dropping signal for a week. The Navy wants it sealed before the weather turns, and the weather is turning, so here I am, talking to a tape recorder in a concrete box.
For the record, the vault rings. I'm getting nearly two seconds of decay off these walls. Which is lovely, and no use to anyone.
The table gives me an hour of low water. Lieutenant Hayes says the Navy's schedule gives two. I'm going with the table. Walt, if you're listening to this over your coffee: I know. I know.
I copied it onto the card, same as you do every morning.
The technician's gone up. Poor lad hated the dark. The hatch seal's salt-crusted solid, I had to put my whole weight on the wheel, and the whole place smells of iron and old rope.
[She works. A ratchet clicks, stops, clicks. Her breath, steady.]
My fingers have stopped being useful. Egg again, Walt. Too much pepper again. It's the only warm thing I've eaten all day, so I've forgiven you.
[Water against the hatch. A sound like a hand laid flat on iron.] That's... earlier than the table said.
The vault sits lower than the bar. I should have put that on the card. And there's a swell under it from the south-east, I can hear the room change. Well.
Four minutes. The splice is nearly sealed. I'll finish, and I'll be out before it matters.
If this recorder ends up on someone else's shelf, tell Walt: the left pocket. He'll know. And tell him I wasn't careless. I read the card.
[The hatch clangs. Static.]
```
