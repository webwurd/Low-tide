### AI DRAFT (reference only)
```text
[Click. The tape wanders a little. A door bangs somewhere in a draught. Wind, high up, against round glass.]
Whoever you are, you've found the locker. Good. Then you've found the lamp room, so I'll say what I'd have wanted said to me.
The lamp's easy once you know her. Wind the weights before you've had your coffee and she'll never let you down. Trim the wick. Never trust the third stair.
And when the fog comes down, listen for the bells before you look for the beam.
I'm leaving the rock tomorrow. Five winters. June would say that's four too many, and she'd be right, and she'd also be the one who stayed.
There are tapes. They're all addressed to her, so forgive the tone. There's one in the cave buoy and one under the bed, if you haven't found them. Listen in order, if you can bear it.
I made them in an order.
I've tried to post them to Ruth Whitcomb eleven times. Wickenden Street, Providence. June's sister. The only person who ever asked me how I was.
I stand at the post office window with the envelope, and I think, she'll hear me grieving, and I'd rather she heard June.
So they're here. If you've a steadier hand than mine, take them to her.
Tom Dunmore left something in the cove hut for me, years ago. A tin, on a crate. I never could bring myself to play it. If you've a stronger stomach than I have, it's yours.
A voice on sixteen asks after me at ten every night. Whoever has the watch. Never more than the weather, and never more than I answer. [A short breath.]
Two grown men, a radio, the same night on both our minds, and neither of us asks the one thing.
Don't do what I did. If somebody's out there, answer them. Talk to a person. A machine is a poor place to keep a life.
The buoy in the cave is still ticking. I don't know who feeds it. Leave it be. Maybe someone needs it more than I did.
[A long pause. The door bangs once.] Mind your head on the gallery door. Goodnight. [Click.]
```
