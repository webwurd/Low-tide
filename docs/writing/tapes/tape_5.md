### AI DRAFT (reference only)
```text
[Click. A diesel engine idling. Gulls. A man clears his throat.]
Boat log. Coast Guard, Gannet Rock run. Monday, fourth of November, nineteen seventy-four. Oh-five-forty. Landed the Pelagic party at the south reef causeway. Mrs.
Haskell, one technician, Lieutenant Hayes.
Wind south-east, rising. Swell building. The table has the bar dry from oh-six-ten to about oh-seven-ten. One hour.
I told the lieutenant. He said the Navy's schedule gave two. I said the table's the table, sir. I said the vault sits lower than the bar and goes under first. He said, noted.
Oh-six-fifty. The technician has come off the reef. Mrs. Haskell has stayed to finish the splice. The lieutenant says she has her time.
Oh-seven-oh-five. Water making on the bar, early, with the swell behind it. I'm holding station off the reef with the engine running. I can see her lamp in the hatch glass.
Oh-seven-ten. Hailing the hatch. No answer. [His voice drops.] Hailing the hatch. Oh God. I can't get in over the bar. The swell's too heavy.
[A pause. Only the engine.]
Oh-seven-twenty-one. Her lamp has gone out.
[A long silence.] This isn't going in the report. The lieutenant is writing his own. I'll say it here instead, for whoever finds it. I said one hour.
Boatswain T. Dunmore, signing off. [A long breath.] I've a girl at home who's four. She'll ask me tonight what I did at work. [Click.]
```
