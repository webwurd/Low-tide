# Details the AI invented

The original design (`docs/narrative_outline.md` before the beta pass) did not include these. They were added while rewriting the tapes and radio lines. The writer is free to keep, change or drop any of them. Items marked **(canon-affecting)** change how another scene reads if dropped.

## Characters
- Arthur's surname **Pell**, age **41**, and his job as an **eighth-grade history and science teacher** from inland Maine.
- Clara's age **28** (27 on tape 6) and her surname **Dunmore**, taken from her father.
- Tom Dunmore was **about 40** in 1974, the same age as Arthur now. **(canon-affecting)** Used in Day 4: "He was about your age that winter."
- Maren is a **reader**, and her cause of death is deliberately left unstated.

## Tapes
- **The card.** Walt copied the tide table onto a card for June every morning, and she kept it in her left pocket. This is the meaning of "tell Walt, the left pocket." **(canon-affecting)**
- **"Don't wait up."** June's last words to Walt at the landing (tape 3).
- **Egg sandwich, too much pepper.** Walt's sandwiches, mentioned on tapes 1 and 2.
- **The vault sits lower than the bar** and floods first. A swell from the south-east brought the water early. **(canon-affecting)**
- **Clara has heard tape 1.** She has serviced the buoy for years and never took Walt's tape out of the hatch. **(canon-affecting)** This is why she lies about the buoy being warm on Day 2.
- **She leaves her own tape under the service hatch** so it doesn't touch Walt's.
- **A voice on sixteen asked after Walt at ten each night.** Clara does the same for Arthur ("I'll be on sixteen at ten, like always").
- **Walt's advice to the next keeper:** "If somebody's out there, answer them."
- **Tape 5's timestamps** (05:40, 06:10-07:10 dry bar, 06:50, 07:05, 07:10, 07:21 "her lamp has gone out") and the closing line "I've a girl at home who's four."
- **Clara signed the battery out** as a spare for the "Penobscot repeater." She could go to prison for a battery.
- **Tape 1's 14-second ping on tape,** counted aloud by Walt.

## Radio
- Clara's **tells**: "Have you eaten?", "Copy," "Say again," turning to weather when a question gets close. Arthur's **tells**: dates and numbers, dry understatement, "I noticed" instead of "I feel."
- Clara calls Arthur **"teacher"** once (Day 1).
- Day 4: Arthur asks Clara **"Have you eaten?"** and gets "...Toast. Don't say it."
- Clara's small slips on the buoy ("That'll be the cold, probably"), the cable ("don't touch the splice") and the vault ("I couldn't have gone down there") hint that she knows more than she says.
- The tide-table card is not yet used anywhere in the epilogues. It would pay off there.
